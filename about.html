e-Waqf Management System

The platform digitises community Waqf collection and aid management for Surau Raudhatul Firdaus. It combines a public contribution portal with secure administration and aid applicant workflows.

Public Portal

Browse verified campaigns, view progress, make simulated online donations and obtain e-receipts.

Aid Applicants

Register, submit aid requests with supporting documents, track progress and manage profiles.

Administration

Review applications, manage campaigns, monitor donations and export financial reports.
